Our Products

Four Ways to Bring Your Marketing Alive

Transforming ordinary physical marketing into interactive digital experiences - no app required.

Halo
Immersive AR Video
Bring your story to life, wherever it is printed. HALO turns flat print and packaging into a window for video. When a customer scans, your brand video plays directly on top of the physical material.
Lumina
3D AR Experiences
Give your products a stage - anywhere, anytime. LUMINA lets customers see products in true 3D, emerging from a poster, package, or print ad. They can rotate, zoom, and place products in their own space before buying.
Seren
Smart 2D AR Stories
Turn static print into stories that unfold. SEREN brings lightweight, story-style AR to packaging, cards, calendars, and books by layering animated visuals, text, or narration over static designs.
Nova
Interactive AR Games
Turn every scan into a conversation. NOVA transforms any printed material - packaging, posters, visiting cards, and in-store displays - into a two-way interactive experience. Customers get to play, choose, and engage with it directly from their phone.

02

03
