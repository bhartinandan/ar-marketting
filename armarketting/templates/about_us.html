About US


Augmented Reality That Speaks for Your Brand. At AliveFrame, we’re on a mission to transform how the world experiences print. We are India’s smartest AR-powered publishing platform — enabling brands, publishers, and marketers to create immersive, interactive experiences directly from posters, packaging, magazines,newspaper and more — all through a simple QR scan image scan. 📲 No app. No hassle. Just scan and experience magic — right in your browser.


Our Vision


To bridge the physical and digital worlds by turning everyday print into meaningful digital experiences. What We Do


AliveFrame lets you:


  • 🎯 Add short videos or interactive 3D content to any printed material

  • 📰 Make newspapers, ads, and packaging “come alive” with AR

  • 📊 Track viewer engagement with real-time analytics

  • 🛠 Manage and deploy campaigns with a simple dashboard

  • Whether you’re a newspaper wanting to boost engagement, or a D2C brand looking to educate customers with packaging-based videos — AliveFrame makes it effortless.
